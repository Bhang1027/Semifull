# Semifull Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Semifull expands YouTube's video player within your browser tab.

## Data handling

Semifull does not collect, store persistently, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts, or remote services.

The extension reads the current YouTube page URL and page structure locally to find the player and add its controls. It listens for keyboard events to support its shortcuts, ignoring G while you type in text fields. It does not record keystrokes, video history, searches, or page content.

Temporary state, such as the current mode and scroll position, exists only in memory while the page is open. Semifull does not use cookies or extension storage.

## Website access

Semifull runs only on https://www.youtube.com/. Its access is used to resize the player, display its button and tooltip, and restore the normal page layout.

YouTube and your browser continue to handle their own data under their respective policies. This policy describes only Semifull.
